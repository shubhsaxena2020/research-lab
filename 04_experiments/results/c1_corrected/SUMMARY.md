## What changes

Threshold caveat: the fitted operating points differ between runs (confirmatory band [0.9072, 0.9478] → [0.5232, 0.5370], exploratory band [0.2974, 0.9478] → [0.3032, 0.6362]), because the thresholds are re-fitted on the corrected dev rows by the same pre-registered rule. The AUROC rows are the threshold-free comparison; the precision/coverage rows compare two different dev-fitted cuts, not the same cut on two feature sets.

No pre-registered verdict changes: H1 is unsupported under both feature sets (rung-1 minus rung-3 abstain precision 0.0 with 95% CI [0.0, 0.0] over 400 paired questions), H2 remains refuted on the within-corpus arm (dev→test loss 0.0007 → 0.0155, both inside the 0.05 refutation band), and H3 remains unsupported — but H3 stops being a degenerate zero and turns significantly negative, -0.0223 (95% CI [-0.0311, -0.0135], n=1479) against 0.0 [0.0, 0.0] in the frozen run, so the band is now measurably worse than the recall-matched single threshold rather than identical to it.

The 'overlap drives the ladder' story survives where it was measurable and fails at rung 4: the surface audit is unchanged between runs (lex-overlap AUROC 0.9773 / 0.8886 / 0.9232 on dev rungs 1-3) and the corrected gate still refuses 1.0000 / 0.9964 / 1.0000 of test rungs 1-3 and 0.9375 of the overlap-matched rung 5, so the ladder is not an artifact of the dense block; what does not survive is rung 4, whose frozen `dense_presented` was really cos(rewrite, gold) (median 0.9926, max 0.9999) and therefore separated 'presented == gold' from 'presented == rewrite' for free — with the §4 question-passage cosine the rewrite scores 0.7352, within noise of its own answerable original, and rung 4 falls to AUROC 0.573 and exploratory-band precision 0.1253.

B2 is now a real baseline rather than a constant: `dense_top1` takes 886 distinct values (it was 1.0 on every rung-0–3 row) and scores 0.9973 pooled abstain precision, but at 0.0027 coverage and 0.9975 over-refusal (dev-feasible=False), so it beats the corrected gate's 0.9743 at 0.0383 coverage only by refusing almost everything — a genuine score at a degenerate operating point, not a usable competitor.
