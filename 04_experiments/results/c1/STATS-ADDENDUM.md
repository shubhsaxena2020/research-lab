### Feature-matrix redundancy (checked, not assumed)

Of the 12 gate columns, 11 are distinct and 10 are informative: identical pairs `dense_presented` ≡ `dense_top1`; constant columns `dense_rank_log` (value 0.693147 = ln 2 in both splits; dev n=1873, test n=1879).
