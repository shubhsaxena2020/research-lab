# MF-17 — AUROC intervals (R3-3b deferred analysis)

**Item:** MF-17 (AUROC intervals + paired difference, recomputed with 10,000 resamples).
**Status:** EXPLORATORY / POST-HOC. This file reports new, independent evidence only. It does not replace, revise, or call confirmatory the frozen R3-3 MF-17 result in `results/c1/r3/exp/auroc_ci.json`.

## Design

- Script: `04_experiments/src/r3_r3_3b_mf17.py` (new lane script).
- Evidence file: `04_experiments/results/c1/r3/R3-3b/mf17_auroc_intervals.json`.
- Source data only; no model retraining beyond the already-specified dev-fitted logistic gates used by the original scripts.
- Bootstrap helpers imported unchanged from `04_experiments/src/r3_exp_auroc_ci.py`: `bootstrap_auroc_ci` and `paired_auroc_diff_ci`.
- `n_boot = 10000`, bootstrap `seed = 20261118`, `alpha = 0.05`, percentile 2.5/97.5 intervals. Audit OOF scores keep the pre-existing 5-fold CV seed 13; the bootstrap seed is 20261118.
- Two resampling levels are preserved and reported side by side: **item** (resample item rows) and **article cluster** (resample whole articles by `title`).
- Item-id alignment is asserted before the paired difference: the frozen and corrected held-out test id sets are equal (1,879 ids), and labels and rungs agree on all common ids. No pairing is computed on unaligned rows.

## Limitations

- Post-hoc/exploratory only. These intervals quantify sampling variability of already-reported point estimates; they do not create a confirmatory test or change the frozen MF-17 verdict.
- Held-out AUROCs use all test rows (answerable rung 0 vs rungs 1–4) from `band_score`; no thresholding or abstention decision is re-fitted.
- Top-1 and transfer gates are refitted on dev as in the original scripts; their intervals condition on that fit and do not include gate-fitting variability.
- The gold indicator is a label-construction artefact: with audit convention `y = 1` for negatives, `is_gold` is 1 for answerables, so the raw is-gold-alone AUROC is 0.0 and its reverse direction is 1.0. The gold-indicator-plus-lexical cell reaching 1.0 is therefore not evidence about rung hardness.
- Article-cluster resampling uses `title`; transfer has 354 clusters, so cluster intervals there are much wider in cluster count but similar numerically to item intervals.
- Audit OOF scores are seed-13 cross-validated predictions; the 10,000-resample bootstrap resamples those fixed scores, it does not refit the audit classifier per replicate.
