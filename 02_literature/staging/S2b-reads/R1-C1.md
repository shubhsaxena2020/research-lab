## Summary

- **No COLLISION.** Nothing in this batch pairs two dev-fitted thresholds with a dead zone, per-negative-hardness-rung held-out evaluation, and per-rung abstain precision/recall/false-refusal.
- The two threshold-shaped neighbours are #3 (retrieval-only score, per-regime abstention, single policy over a coverage sweep) and #6 (retrieval-side gate that does report wrongful refusals, single calibrated cut); #8 is the closest on the hardness/negative-construction axis.
- The unoccupied core is the *hardness ladder itself* (random → same-entity → same-topic near-miss → premise violation) with a dead zone, its over-refusal rate per rung, and where the two thresholds fall in the dev feature range.
