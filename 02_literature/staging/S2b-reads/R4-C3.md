# R4-C3 — Reads against slice C3 (fallback topic)

**Slice under test:** published operating-point table reporting precision, recall **and** alert volume (alerts per 10,000 messages) at several base rates in 0.1%–1%, for a small CPU-runnable detector, with the decision threshold set from a **stated cost ratio** (FP cost vs FN cost), on a **code-mixed Indian-English SMS** corpus.

**Verdict legend:** COLLISION = publishes that table; CLOSE = occupies a large part of it (named below); ADJACENT = imbalanced learning / robustness without deployment-prevalence framing; IRRELEVANT = not about detection thresholding.

## Notes on method
- All quotes are verbatim from the text read (batch file, which carries the OpenAlex abstracts); each was string-matched programmatically against the source abstract. None exceeds 25 words.
- Fetches used: 1 (`https://doi.org/10.18653/2026.groundlm-1.79`, paper 3) — resolved to a landing page restating the abstract, adding no new operating-point detail. No other fetches were needed; every remaining verdict is settled by the abstract as printed.
- No paper reports alerts per 10,000 messages. No paper states a cost ratio (FP:FN) used to *choose* the threshold. Papers 7 and 8 come closest on economics but are credit-card, not SMS; papers 2 and 3 come closest on domain but are FPR-gated, not cost-ratio-gated.
