**Task:** Recompute MF-17 AUROC intervals from existing per-item predictions with fixed seed 20261118 and ≥10,000 bootstrap resamples; report intervals for frozen/corrected held-out AUROC and their paired difference over common held-out items, top-1 AUROC, transfer AUROC, and gold-indicator/lexical audit AUROC; preserve item vs article-cluster resampling; all post-hoc/exploratory.

## What was done

1. Implemented the script using the unmodified bootstrap helpers `bootstrap_auroc_ci` and `paired_auroc_diff_ci` from `04_experiments/src/r3_exp_auroc_ci.py`, with `n_boot=10000`, bootstrap `seed=20261118`, `alpha=0.05`.
2. Asserted item-id alignment before the paired difference: 1,879 frozen test rows, 1,879 corrected test rows, 1,879 common ids; label and rung vectors identical on the common ids; all ids have article titles (18 articles). Assertions are recorded in `alignment_assertions` and are hard failures.
3. Computed item-level and article-cluster-level percentile CIs for: frozen and corrected held-out (test) AUROC; paired frozen−corrected difference; top-1 presented test AUROC; transfer AUROC (both frozen and corrected gates); lexical audit and gold-indicator-plus-lexical audit (dev rungs 1–4).
4. Recorded input paths and sha256, exact command, seed, n_boot, alpha, denominators, resampling description, post-hoc label, and limitations in the JSON; emitted a flat prefixed `results` object plus detailed nested sections.
5. Ran the generator and captured stdout (below). Ran `--check-repro` (outcome below).

## Limitations / caveats

- Post-hoc/exploratory; no confirmatory claim and no change to the frozen MF-17 verdict.
- Intervals on top-1 and transfer AUROC condition on dev-fitted gates; gate-fitting variability is not resampled.
- The gold-indicator cell is a construction artefact (raw is-gold-alone AUROC 0.0, reverse direction 1.0 under the audit's negative-as-positive convention); its 1.0 CI is not evidence about rung hardness.
- Audit OOF scores use the existing seed-13 5-fold construction; bootstrap resamples those fixed scores.
- Article-cluster resampling is reported alongside item-level resampling, not substituted for it.
