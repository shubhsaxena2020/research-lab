## Deliberate scope limits (also stated in ORIGINALITY.md and in every report)

- Not a plagiarism detector; a pass only means no run of ≥8 words from *this* corpus
  survives outside quotation marks. It does not prove originality.
- No network, no upload, no third-party service: the draft never leaves the machine.
- Cannot see synonym-level paraphrase, ideas, structure, unarchived sources, figures, or
  self-plagiarism; the manual top-10 read is mandatory and is the part that catches those.
- `--min-words` above `--n` intentionally hides shorter runs; the pass gate uses n=8.
